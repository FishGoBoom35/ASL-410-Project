---Explanation---
Basically instead of prompting the with all the information at once at the start, the AI is only given
information that is relevant to the topic being asked. This should help keep the llm on topic.


Information folder - follow the format of the books file. Keywords are what is being looked for in the
sign. Example: Sign: **book** order how vs **textbook** order how will get information from the same file.
The line after the keywords line is where all your information goes about whatever sign goes. As we add
new signs, just add them to their own .txt file with keywords and the information and you should be good
to go.

Prompts folder - Basically just ignore this, it is the instructions the AI uses to know how to respond
and the examples file is just what a typical conversation between the user and AI looks like.

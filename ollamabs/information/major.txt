UTampa offers more than 200 programs of study, including majors, minors and pre-professional programs.
The four colleges are: College of Arts and Letters; Sykes College of Business; College of Natural and Health Sciences; College of Social Sciences, Mathematics and Education.
Popular majors include business, marketing, and communication and media studies.
How to declare or change a major: submit the request in Workday. Students must officially declare a major by the time they have earned 60 credit hours.
Help choosing a major: meet with the academic exploration advisor at the Academic Success Center, Technology Building 2nd floor, (813) 257-5757, academicsuccess@ut.edu.
Change your academic advisor: fill out the change request form on SpartanNet.
Undeclared students: about 30 percent of freshmen start undeclared and should meet the exploration advisor during their first semester.
